Snake

A masked Double + Dueling DQN guided by a multi-ply look-ahead search — watch it play or play it yourself, both running entirely in your browser (no server). The learned net evaluates moves; a flood-fill survival search plans several steps ahead to avoid boxing itself in, so it fills much more of the board than the net alone. The Hamiltonian cycle mode goes further: the snake always keeps a cycle it provably cannot die on and lets the net pick safe shortcuts along it — every game ends with the board completely full.

Even sizes only — the Hamiltonian cycle exists only on a board with an even edge. Changing the size restarts the current game; changing the speed does not. On a big board the look-ahead search runs shallower to keep the tick fast, so “Watch AI” plays a little looser and tops out near 8 moves/sec at 50×50 however high you set the speed. The Hamiltonian mode is unaffected — it stays fast at every size and still fills the whole board.
