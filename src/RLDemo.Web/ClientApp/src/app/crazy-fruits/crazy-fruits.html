Crazy Fruits

A match-3 in the spirit of the Flash-era KidCity classic — swap two adjacent fruits to line up 3 or more of the same kind. Match 4 for a striped fruit (clears its line), an L or T for a wrapped fruit (double explosion), 5 in a row for a sugar bomb (swap it with anything to clear that fruit) — and combine specials for the big blasts. Rounds are 30 moves. Runs entirely in your browser.
