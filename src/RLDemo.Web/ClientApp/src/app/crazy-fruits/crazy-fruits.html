Crazy Fruits

A match-3 in the spirit of the Flash-era KidCity classic — swap two adjacent fruits to line up 3 or more of the same kind. Matches clear, fruit tumbles down, and chain reactions multiply the points. Drag a fruit toward its neighbour, or tap one and then tap where it should go. Runs entirely in your browser.
